How does image compression work?

Image compression reduces the file size of an image while attempting to preserve as much visual quality as possible.

This tool processes your image directly in your browser. Your image is not uploaded to a server.

Why compress images?

  • Reduce website loading times
  • Save storage space
  • Make images easier to send
  • Reduce email attachment sizes
